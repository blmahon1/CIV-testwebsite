About Us

Our History

At TipToe Soles, we believe that comfort and style should never come at a high cost. Based in Sydney, Australia, we've been crafting high-quality footwear as a white-label manufacturer for years. Now, we're stepping out with our own brand, delivering affordable, durable, and stylish daily wear shoes designed for customers aged 30 and above. Our mission is simple: to become the go-to footwear brand for comfort and affordability, both in Australia and around the world.

Our Team

Our team at TipToe Soles is made up of passionate designers, skilled craftsmen, and dedicated professionals who share a vision for redefining footwear comfort and style. With years of experience in the industry, we work tirelessly to ensure every pair of shoes we create meets the highest standards of quality and affordability. Together, we are committed to delivering products that keep our customers stepping confidently, every day.
